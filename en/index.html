Frequently

Asked

Will the result look natural?

That is our aim. We dose to suit you and tell you honestly when a treatment is not right for you. The goal is for you to look refreshed – not “done”.

What if I don’t like the result?

We deliberately dose with restraint – adding a little more is always possible. Hyaluronic acid fillers can also be dissolved if needed. So you stay on the safe side.

How much does the consultation cost?

Before or during a treatment the consultation is free. As a separate appointment without treatment it costs CHF 50. We take time for your questions and explain every option clearly.

Who carries out the treatments?

The owner herself: a naturopath (Heilpraktikerin), nurse and medical cosmetologist with extensive further training in aesthetic medicine – self-employed since 2009, with many years of experience working alongside plastic surgeons.

Is the treatment painful?

Most treatments are very tolerable. Where it helps, we use numbing cream or cooling. We explain beforehand exactly what you will feel.

Can I go out straight after the treatment?

With many treatments, straight away or after a few hours. Mild redness or swelling is possible depending on the method – we discuss this with you beforehand.

Which languages do you speak?

German, English, French, Italian, Russian and Ukrainian – our clientele is very international.
