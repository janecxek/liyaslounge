Get in touch

Will the result look natural?

That is our aim. We dose to suit you and tell you honestly when a treatment is not right for you. The goal is for you to look refreshed – not “done”.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The initial consultation is free and without obligation. We take time for your questions and explain every option clearly.

Is the treatment painful?

Most treatments are very tolerable. Where it helps, we use numbing cream or cooling. We explain beforehand exactly what you will feel.

Can I go out straight after the treatment?

With many treatments, straight away or after a few hours. Mild redness or swelling is possible depending on the method. You will find details on each treatment page.

How do I book an appointment?

How much does a treatment cost?
