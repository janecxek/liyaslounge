Your questions

Honestly answered

An aesthetic treatment is a matter of trust. Here you will find answers to the most important questions about your visit.

Will the result look natural – or will I look changed?

Our highest goal is to enhance your natural beauty – never to change you. Thanks to our specialisation in holistic face balancing, we always look at your proportions as a whole. We work on the principle of “less is more”, so you look fresh and rested – and completely like yourself.

I don’t know yet which treatment suits me – is that a problem?

Not at all – that is exactly what we are here for. You do not need to bring a ready-made solution. In a thorough consultation we analyse your skin and your facial or body structure and listen carefully. Together we create a tailored plan that fits your wishes and your budget.

Is a treatment painful?

Your comfort comes first. Many of our device-based and regenerative treatments are very gentle anyway. For more intensive treatments such as fillers, mesotherapy or Morpheus8 we apply an effective, soothing numbing cream beforehand – so the treatment is as pleasant and pain-free as possible.

How soon can I go out after the treatment?

Most treatments fit easily into everyday life. Depending on the method, mild redness, slight swelling or tiny injection marks may be visible straight afterwards; they usually fade within 24 to 72 hours. In the consultation we tell you openly about any downtime so you can plan your appointment.

Can different treatments be combined?

Yes – a gentle combination of methods often gives the most harmonious and lasting results, for example deep-acting skin boosters for skin quality together with targeted face balancing. We coordinate every step perfectly in timing and expertise.

Which products and devices do you use?

We make no compromises on safety and quality. We follow the strictest medical hygiene standards, use state-of-the-art devices and rely exclusively on certified, biocompatible premium products from South Korea, known for their purity and excellent tolerability.

Are men welcome too?

Of course. More and more men appreciate discreet, natural aesthetics for a fresh, vital appearance. Because male anatomy and skin structure have their own characteristics, we adapt every treatment individually to male proportions.

What if I don’t like the result?

We deliberately dose with restraint – adding a little more is always possible. Hyaluronic acid fillers can also be dissolved if needed. And we keep supporting you personally until you are fully satisfied.

How much does the consultation cost?

Before or during a treatment the consultation is free. As a separate appointment without treatment it costs CHF 50. We take time for your questions and explain every option clearly.

How much does a treatment cost?

For example, teeth whitening from CHF 80, dermal fillers from CHF 170 or HIFU from CHF 200. You will find all prices on the individual treatment pages; we confirm the final cost honestly in your consultation.

How do I arrange an appointment?

Directly online through our booking system – around the clock. Or via WhatsApp, voice message or Instagram. We do not take phone calls, because during treatments we are fully there for our clients – if needed, we are happy to call you back.

Where is Liyas Lounge?

Liyas Lounge, Hammerallee 23, 4600 Olten – on the first lower floor (bell “Liyas Lounge & Lala Lashes”). We are happy to send you directions via WhatsApp.

Which languages do you speak?

German, English, French, Italian, Russian and Ukrainian – our clientele is very international.

How can I pay?

Payment is made directly after the treatment – in cash, with TWINT or by card.

Your journey

Step by step

Transparency, safety and your well-being are the basis of our work. So that you feel relaxed and in the best hands from the very first moment, we guide you through clear, carefully coordinated steps – without any rush.

  1. I
    01Personal consultation & medical history

    Everything starts with a thorough, honest conversation in a calm atmosphere. We listen to you, look at your face or body as a whole and discuss your wishes. A detailed medical questionnaire clarifies your history and any intolerances – for maximum safety.

  2. II
    02Gentle preparation

    Your comfort matters to us. We prepare your skin gently and, depending on the treatment and how you feel, apply a soothing numbing cream – so that everything is as pleasant and pain-free as possible.

  3. III
    03Your tailored treatment

    With medical precision, an artistic eye and plenty of calm, we carry out the treatment we planned together. On request we explain every step – always with an eye on natural proportions, soft transitions and first-class hygiene.

  4. IV
    04Aftercare & care advice

    Straight afterwards you receive a soothing finishing treatment and clear, easy-to-follow advice for the next few days at home. This supports your recovery and lets your result develop perfectly.

  5. V
    05Follow-up until you are fully satisfied

    Our care does not end at the door. We stay in touch, ask how you are and arrange a check-up if needed. Only when the result has healed harmoniously and you are happy with what you see in the mirror is our work complete.

Do you have questions about a treatment or the process beforehand? Message us any time – we are happy to take time for you.
