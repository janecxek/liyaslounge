How long do dermal fillers last?

That depends on the product, the area treated and your metabolism – usually around 6 to 12 months. In areas with a lot of movement, such as the lips, often a little less.

Is the treatment painful?

Will people be able to tell?

Our aim is a natural, harmonious result. Some swelling or minor bruising may appear in the first few days and usually settles on its own.

Thread Lift with PDO threads

Fine, absorbable threads gently lift the tissue and stimulate collagen – for more definition without a scalpel.

  • 30–60 min
  • Ø 12–18 months*
  • 1–3 days downtime

About the treatment

PDO (polydioxanone) threads are placed under the skin through fine cannulas. So-called cog threads have small barbs that gently reposition the tissue. As the threads dissolve over several months, they stimulate the body’s own collagen production.

Areas of treatment

  • Non-surgical nose correction
  • Cheeks and mid-face

How long does a thread lift last?

Usually 12 to 18 months. The threads dissolve after a few months, but the effect lasts longer thanks to collagen production.
