Plasma Pen Olten

What is the Plasma Pen?

Fine plasma pulses tighten the skin’s surface in a targeted way – around the eyelids, the mouth or on the neck, for example.

The Plasma Pen works without direct contact with the skin: a fine electrical arc creates tiny points on the skin’s surface, which then contract. This tightens the tissue and stimulates regeneration. At Liyas Lounge in Olten we mainly use it for small, delicate areas.

A small plasma arc forms between the tip of the device and the skin. It creates precise micro-points that make the skin contract and stimulate collagen. The points form tiny crusts that fall off on their own after a few days.

  • Targeted tightening of small areas
  • No scalpel and no stitches
  • For eyelids, mouth, neck and body areas

Who it’s for

Areas of treatment

  • Hooded eyelids and the eye area
  • Fine lines around the mouth
  • Cheeks and forehead
  • Neck and décolleté
  • Knees, abdomen and upper arms

Good to know

Not suitable for

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • A tendency to keloid scarring
  • Acute skin inflammation or cold sores
  • Very dark skin types (risk of pigmentation)

Whether a treatment is right for you is something we discuss in your personal consultation.

How long does healing take?

The tiny crusts usually fall off after 5 to 7 days. During this time, treat the skin gently and protect it from the sun.

Is the Plasma Pen painful?

With numbing cream the treatment is very tolerable. A brief feeling of warmth is normal.

When will I see the result?

A first effect is visible once the skin has healed, and the tightening continues to develop over several weeks.

How much does the Plasma Pen cost in Olten?

A small zone costs CHF 200, a large zone CHF 300.
