Filler Correction

Is this for you?

Not happy with a previous filler result – perhaps it looks too full, uneven or has migrated? Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved in a targeted way. At Liyas Lounge in Olten we assess the situation carefully and only treat as much as necessary.

Suitable for

  • Overfilled lips or cheeks
  • Lumps or unevenness after filler
  • Asymmetry
  • Migrated or visible filler, e.g. in the tear trough
  • Wanting a more natural fresh start

How does filler correction work?

An enzyme is injected precisely into the area with the hyaluronic acid filler and breaks it down within a short time. This makes it possible to correct overfilling, lumps or asymmetry – also as preparation for a new, more balanced treatment.

  • Corrects too much or uneven filler
  • Works on hyaluronic acid fillers only
  • The basis for a fresh start with a natural result

Filler Correction

Frequently asked

Can every filler be dissolved?

Only fillers based on hyaluronic acid. Other materials do not react to the enzyme – we clarify this in the analysis.

Will my own hyaluronic acid be broken down too?

Your body constantly produces new hyaluronic acid. We dose precisely and as little as possible.

When can I have filler again?

Usually after about two weeks, once the tissue has settled.

How much does filler correction cost in Olten?

The treatment costs CHF 200. We discuss the exact scope in your consultation.
