Dermal Fillers

Frequently asked

What are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are gels based on hyaluronic acid – a substance found naturally in our skin, where it helps to retain moisture. Depending on the area and the goal, they are placed with a fine needle or cannula at different depths of the skin. We use approved products only and choose the consistency and quantity to suit your face.

How long do dermal fillers last?

That depends on the product, the area treated and your metabolism – usually around 6 to 12 months. In areas with a lot of movement, such as the lips, often a little less.

Is the treatment painful?

Many fillers contain a local anaesthetic, and a numbing cream can also be applied. Most clients find the treatment very tolerable.

Will people be able to tell?

Our aim is a natural, harmonious result. Some swelling or minor bruising may appear in the first few days and usually settles on its own.

Can fillers be dissolved?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme if needed – for example to correct a result. That gives you extra peace of mind.

How much do dermal fillers cost in Olten?

At Liyas Lounge, fillers start from CHF 170 for 0.5 ml, 1 ml costs CHF 250. Full-face packages with wrinkle treatment start from CHF 500. We discuss the right quantity and price honestly in your consultation.

Results & Longevity
  • Visible immediately; the final shape settles within a few days
  • Lasts approx. 6–12 months depending on product and area
  • Mild swelling or minor bruising usually settles quickly

*Results and longevity vary from person to person and cannot be guaranteed.

When is the treatment not suitable?
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Acute inflammation or infection in the treatment area
  • Known allergy to any of the ingredients
  • Unrealistic expectations

Whether a treatment is right for you is something we discuss in your personal consultation.
