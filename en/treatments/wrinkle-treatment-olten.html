Wrinkle Treatment

Is this for you?

Forehead lines, frown lines and crow’s feet are caused by repeated muscle movement. The wrinkle treatment reduces the activity of these muscles in a targeted way with fine injections. At Liyas Lounge in Olten we dose with deliberate restraint – you should look rested, never expressionless.

Areas of treatment

  • Forehead lines
  • Frown lines between the brows
  • Crow’s feet
  • Cat eyes & Nefertiti lift (neck and jawline)
  • Masseter (face slimming) and shoulder line
  • Excessive underarm sweating

Wrinkle Treatment

Frequently asked

How does the wrinkle treatment work?

Small amounts of a preparation are injected into selected muscles. The muscles relax and the skin above them smooths out. The effect begins after a few days and gradually wears off after a few months – so the treatment is easy to plan and adjust.

When will I see the effect?

The effect usually begins after 3 to 7 days, with the full effect visible after around two weeks.

How long does it last?

Usually 3 to 4 months. With regular treatments, the effect may last a little longer for some people.

Will I look expressionless?

No – we deliberately dose individually and with restraint, so your natural expression is preserved.

How much does the wrinkle treatment cost in Olten?

One zone costs CHF 150, three zones CHF 350 and full face CHF 650 – the follow-up check after 2–3 weeks is included. In the consultation before your treatment we discuss what makes sense for you.

When is the treatment not suitable?
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Neuromuscular conditions
  • Acute inflammation in the treatment area
  • Known intolerance to the active ingredient

Whether a treatment is right for you is something we discuss in your personal consultation.
