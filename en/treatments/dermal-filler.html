The product

What are dermal fillers?

Dermal fillers are gels based on hyaluronic acid – a substance found naturally in our skin, where it helps to retain moisture. Placed precisely in specific layers of the skin, they can replace lost volume, define contours or soften fine lines.

We use approved products only, and choose the consistency and quantity to suit each area and your goals. More often than not, less is more.

Areas of treatment

Possible areas

  • Lips – contour, symmetry and hydration
  • Nasolabial folds and marionette lines
  • Cheeks and mid-face
  • Chin and jawline
  • Tear trough – only after careful assessment

Whether an area is suitable for you is something we discuss during your personal consultation.

The process

Step by step

  1. Consultation

    We talk about your wishes, your health and what can realistically be achieved – free of charge and without obligation.

  2. Planning

    Together we agree on the areas, product and quantity. You receive full information on costs and possible side effects.

  3. Treatment

    After disinfection, and local anaesthesia if you wish, the filler is placed using fine needles or cannulas.

  4. Aftercare

    You receive clear aftercare advice. If you like, we arrange a review appointment after around two weeks.

Good to know

After your treatment

Mild swelling, redness or minor bruising may occur during the first few days. These usually settle on their own. For 24–48 hours we recommend avoiding exercise, saunas, alcohol and firm pressure on the treated area.

Not suitable for

When we advise against it

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Acute inflammation or infection in the treatment area
  • Known allergy to any of the ingredients
  • Certain autoimmune or blood-clotting conditions
  • Unrealistic expectations – something we will always address honestly
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the treatment painful?

Everyone experiences it differently. Many fillers contain a local anaesthetic, and a numbing cream can also be applied if you wish.

How long does a filler last?

That depends on the product, the area treated and your metabolism. Hyaluronic acid is gradually broken down by the body – you will receive a realistic estimate during your consultation.

Will people be able to tell I have had treatment?

Our aim is a natural, harmonious result. Some swelling or minor bruising may appear in the first few days and usually settles on its own.

Can a filler be reversed?

In many cases, hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme (hyaluronidase). Whether this is appropriate is assessed on an individual basis.
