Teeth Whitening

Frequently asked

What is Teeth Whitening?

A whitening gel is applied to the teeth and activated with light. It lifts surface discolouration and makes the teeth look brighter. Power whitening uses several rounds for a more intensive effect.

How white will my teeth become?

That depends on your starting shade and the type of discolouration. We tell you honestly in advance what is realistic.

Is whitening harmful to teeth?

With healthy teeth and correct application, cosmetic whitening is considered gentle. Temporary sensitivity is possible.

What should I avoid afterwards?

For the first 48 hours, avoid coffee, tea, red wine and smoking where possible.

How much does teeth whitening cost in Olten?

Soft whitening costs CHF 80, power whitening CHF 120.

Results & Care
  • Result visible straight after the treatment
  • Avoid staining foods and drinks for the first 48 hours
  • Longevity depends on diet and habits

*Results and longevity vary from person to person and cannot be guaranteed.

When is the treatment not suitable?
  • Tooth decay or gum inflammation
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Very sensitive teeth
  • Crowns or fillings in the visible area (these do not lighten)

If you have any dental issues, we recommend a check-up with your dentist first.
