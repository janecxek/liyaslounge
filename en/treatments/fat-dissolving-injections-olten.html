Fat-dissolving Injections

Is this for you?

Some fat deposits remain stubborn despite exercise and a balanced diet – on the chin, the abdomen or the hips, for example. Injection lipolysis is one way of treating such small areas in a targeted manner. At Liyas Lounge in Olten we will tell you honestly whether the method is right for you.

Areas of treatment

  • Double chin and jawline
  • Small fat deposits on the abdomen
  • Hips and saddlebags
  • Upper arms and knees
  • Cellulite

What is Injection Lipolysis?

An active ingredient is injected into the subcutaneous fat, where it targets fat cells. The body then breaks down the released components through its natural metabolism. The method is suited to small, defined fat deposits – it is not a weight-loss treatment.

  • Targeted at small, stubborn fat deposits
  • No surgery or general anaesthetic
  • For double chin, abdomen, hips and thighs

Frequently asked

Is lipolysis a way to lose weight?

No. Lipolysis is suited to small, stubborn fat deposits – not to weight loss.

How many sessions are needed?

Depending on the area, usually 2 to 4 sessions a few weeks apart.

What will I feel afterwards?

Swelling, redness or a feeling of warmth for a few days is common. This is normal and usually settles on its own.

How much does lipolysis cost in Olten?

One zone (1 ampoule) costs CHF 200, two zones CHF 350 and three zones CHF 450. For larger areas there is a package of 10 for CHF 1000.
