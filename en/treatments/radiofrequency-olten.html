Is this for you?

Radiofrequency

Frequently asked

What is radiofrequency?

High-frequency energy warms the deeper layers of the skin without injuring the surface. The collagen fibres contract and the body forms new collagen – over the following weeks the skin looks firmer and smoother. Most clients find the treatment pleasantly warm.

Is radiofrequency painful?

No – most clients find the warmth pleasant and relaxing.

How is it different from HIFU and Morpheus8?

HIFU works with focused ultrasound in deep layers, Morpheus8 combines radiofrequency with fine needles. Pure radiofrequency is the gentlest option – with no needles at all.

How many sessions do I need?

We usually recommend a course of 4 to 6 sessions, 1 to 2 weeks apart.

How much does radiofrequency cost in Olten?

The price depends on the zone and the number of sessions. We are happy to advise you personally.

Results & Course
  • A fresher complexion often straight after the treatment
  • Increasing firmness over the following weeks
  • Best results usually with a course of several sessions

*Results and longevity vary from person to person and cannot be guaranteed.

When is the treatment not suitable?
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemakers or metal implants in the area
  • Acute skin inflammation
  • Fever or acute infections

Whether a treatment is right for you is something we discuss in your personal consultation.
