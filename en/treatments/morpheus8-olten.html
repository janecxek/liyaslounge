Morpheus8

Frequently asked

What is Morpheus8?

A handpiece with fine, insulated needles reaches adjustable depths and releases radiofrequency heat there. The tissue is warmed in a targeted way, encouraging new collagen and elastin – for smoother, firmer-looking skin.

How many sessions do I need?

We usually recommend 3 sessions, 4 to 6 weeks apart – which is why we offer packages of 3 at a reduced price.

Is Morpheus8 painful?

With numbing cream the treatment is very tolerable. You will feel warmth and a prickling sensation.

How long will I be red?

Usually 1 to 3 days. Make-up can generally be worn again after 24 hours.

How much does Morpheus8 cost in Olten?

Small zones cost CHF 200, the full face CHF 390 and larger body zones CHF 450. When booking several zones at once, you get 50% off the second zone.

Results & Sessions
  • Improved skin after the very first session
  • Best results after 3 sessions, 4–6 weeks apart
  • Redness usually settles within 1–3 days

*Results and longevity vary from person to person and cannot be guaranteed.

When is the treatment not suitable?
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemakers or metal implants in the area
  • Acute skin inflammation or cold sores
  • A fresh tan or sunburn

Whether a treatment is right for you is something we discuss in your personal consultation.
