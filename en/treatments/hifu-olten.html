HIFU 5D Lift

Is this for you?

HIFU stands for High Intensity Focused Ultrasound. The energy is directed precisely into deeper tissue layers without damaging the skin’s surface. At Liyas Lounge in Olten we use it for the face, neck and décolleté as well as for body areas.

Areas of treatment

  • Forehead, cheeks and jawline
  • Neck and décolleté
  • Upper arms
  • Abdomen and waist
  • Thighs

What is HIFU 5D?

Focused ultrasound creates small thermal points at defined depths. The body responds by producing new collagen – the tissue becomes firmer and tighter over the following weeks. The treatment is non-invasive and requires no downtime.

  • Tightening without needles or incisions
  • For face, neck, décolleté and body
  • Natural results through collagen production

Frequently asked

Is HIFU painful?

You will feel warmth or tingling during the pulses. Most clients find the treatment very bearable.

When will I see results?

A first effect is often visible straight away. The full effect appears after 2 to 3 months, as new collagen needs time to form.

Is there any downtime?

No. Mild redness is possible and usually fades within a few hours.

How much does HIFU cost in Olten?

One zone costs CHF 150, a full face lift CHF 300 and face, neck & décolleté CHF 500. Body zones from CHF 200.
