HIFU 5D Lift

Frequently asked

What is HIFU 5D?

Focused ultrasound creates small thermal points at defined depths. The body responds by producing new collagen – the tissue becomes firmer and tighter over the following weeks. The treatment is non-invasive and requires no downtime.

Is HIFU painful?

You will feel warmth or tingling during the pulses. Most clients find the treatment very bearable.

When will I see results?

A first effect is often visible straight away. The full effect appears after 2 to 3 months, as new collagen needs time to form.

Is there any downtime?

No. Mild redness is possible and usually fades within a few hours.

How much does HIFU cost in Olten?

Small zones such as the double chin or eyelids cost CHF 200, a full face lift CHF 300 and face, neck & décolleté CHF 450. Body zones from CHF 250.

Results & Longevity
  • A first effect straight after the treatment
  • Full effect after 2–3 months through collagen production
  • A top-up is usually advisable after 6–12 months

*Results and longevity vary from person to person and cannot be guaranteed.

When is the treatment not suitable?
  • Pregnancy
  • Metal implants in the treatment area
  • Open wounds or skin inflammation
  • Pacemakers

Whether a treatment is right for you is something we discuss in your personal consultation.
