Mesotherapy

Is this for you?

Tired, dull or dehydrated skin? Mesotherapy delivers high-quality active ingredients such as hyaluronic acid, polynucleotides or exosomes directly into the skin – exactly where they are needed. At Liyas Lounge in Olten we choose the preparation to suit your skin and your goals.

Areas of treatment

  • Dry, tired or dull skin
  • Fine lines and loss of elasticity
  • Eye area (e.g. Ami Eyes)
  • Neck and décolleté
  • Hair loss and thinning hair (scalp)
  • Body: skin tightening and cellulite

What is Mesotherapy?

Very fine needles are used to place small amounts of an active-ingredient blend into the upper layers of the skin. Depending on the preparation, this supports hydration, skin structure or natural regeneration. For the best results we usually recommend a course of several sessions.

  • More hydration and elasticity
  • A fresher, more even complexion
  • For face, neck, décolleté, scalp and body

Mesotherapy

Frequently asked

How many sessions do I need?

That depends on the preparation and your skin. We usually recommend a course of 2 to 4 sessions, a few weeks apart.

Is mesotherapy painful?

The needles are very fine. With numbing cream, most clients find the treatment comfortable.

How is it different from dermal fillers?

Fillers add targeted volume and contour. Mesotherapy and skin boosters improve skin quality – hydration, elasticity and glow – without adding volume.

How much does mesotherapy cost in Olten?

A small zone (1 ml) costs CHF 250, a larger zone (2 ml) CHF 350. Skin boosters such as polynucleotides, Korean Glass Skin or Profhilo cost CHF 350, exosomes CHF 450.
